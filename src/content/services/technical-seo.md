Technical SEO focuses on the backend architecture of your website. We ensure that search engine bots can easily access, crawl, and interpret your website without encountering technical roadblocks.

### Core Focus Areas

- **Site Speed & Core Web Vitals:** Optimizing load times and interactive performance.
- **Indexability & Crawl Budget:** Managing robots.txt, XML sitemaps, and resolving crawl errors.
- **Structured Data:** Implementing schema markup to enhance rich snippets in search results.
- **Security:** Ensuring HTTPS and secure browsing experiences.
